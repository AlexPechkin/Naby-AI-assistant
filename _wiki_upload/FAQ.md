# FAQ

**Is Naby free?**
Yes, for personal, family and study use. For work and business there is a [commercial license](License-and-Pricing) from $10 a year.

**Do I need API keys?**
No. Without keys, Naby uses the ChatGPT, Claude and Kimi desktop apps if you have them, free web chats and its local model. Keys make the answers stronger.

**Does it work offline?**
Partly. The local model, folder search, OCR of scans, meeting transcription and many document tasks work without internet. Cloud models, web tasks and updates need a connection.

**Will it slow down my computer?**
You choose: 30 % (the default), 50 % or 70 % of CPU and memory. While you type or move the mouse, Naby waits.

**Do I need a graphics card?**
No. The small LFM3 models run on an ordinary CPU. At 70 % power, a graphics card is used if you have one.

**Where is my data?**
On your computer, and the memory is encrypted. Before anything is sent to a cloud model, personal details are masked. See [Privacy and security](Privacy-and-Security).

**Can Naby break something or delete my files?**
Deletions go to Naby's trash for 30 days, and every strong action is confirmed separately. “What I did today” shows everything, and any step can be undone.

**Which languages does Naby speak?**
It asks for your language on the first start and switches the whole interface to it, including English, Russian and Chinese.

**How does it update?**
By itself. Each update is signed by the author and verified before it is installed. See the [Changelog](Changelog).

**Can I make my own profession?**
Yes. See [Professions → Community professions](Professions#community-professions-build-50).

**Something doesn't work. What do I do?**
Tell Naby in the chat: it will ask a stronger model what went wrong and try to fix itself. Or press **🐞 Bug report**. You can also write to solpvo.tech@gmail.com.
