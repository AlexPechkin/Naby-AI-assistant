## Getting started

Naby lives on your computer and grows by talking with you.

1. Just write to it in the window — it will answer and learn from every message. ([Chatting and learning](#chatting-and-learning))
2. Need some work done (summarize a document, find something, make something)? Turn on the “Task” switch under the input box. ([Tasks for AI models](#tasks-for-ai-models))
3. Give 👍 or 👎 under answers — that's how it learns whom to trust and when to answer on its own.
4. Have keys for ChatGPT/Claude/GigaChat? Add them and it gets smarter. It works without keys too. ([Keys for AI models](#keys-for-ai-models))
5. Smart home in one click: it finds your lamps and smart plugs and follows spoken commands. ([Smart home](#smart-home))

6. What's arrived in the latest builds — [new in Naby](#new-in-naby-builds-4043).

## Who Naby is

Naby isn't an ordinary program, and it isn't just a chatbot. It's a growing mind on your computer.

- It has its own small neural network that keeps learning all its life and remembers you.
- It picks and connects other AI models for each task by itself — you don't need to figure out which one is for what. ([Tasks for AI models](#tasks-for-ai-models))
- When it can't do something yet, it says “let me learn how” rather than “I can't”, and looks for a way.
- Everything it has learned about you stays with you, encrypted. ([Data and privacy](#data-and-privacy))
- It has unbreakable laws: do no harm, obey its person, and be honest about what it is. ([Naby's laws](#nabys-laws))

## Chatting and learning

Write to Naby as you would to a person. It answers at its current stage of development and learns from every message.

- 📎 — attach a file (or drag it into the window, or paste it with Ctrl+V).
- Esc — stop an answer. Ctrl+N — new chat, Ctrl+K — search your chats.
- ✎ at the top — instructions for the whole chat: role, style, language.
- 👍/👎 under answers teach it whom to trust. ([👍 and 👎](#-and-))

## Tasks for AI models

The “Task” switch under the input box turns your message into a task: Naby picks suitable AI models by itself (apps on your computer, free web chats or your keys) and gives you one finished answer.

- Next to it you can choose who answers: “Naby decides”, Claude, ChatGPT, Kimi, all three, or an AI website.
- “Auto-OK” — allow my changes in this chat without asking (except those marked ⚠).
- It sees a task through to the end and carries on after a restart. ([Seeing tasks through](#seeing-tasks-through))

## Seeing tasks through

After each task, Naby checks for itself: did it do what you wanted? If not, it tries another way.

- If one AI model said “impossible”, Naby tells you which one and asks the others.
- All tasks are written to a log: after a restart it picks up where it left off. “my tasks” («мои задачи») shows what's in progress.
- Say “stop” («стоп») and it stops.

## 👍 and 👎

Ratings under answers are the main way to help me grow. 👍 means “answer like this”, 👎 means “not what I wanted”.
Over time they teach me your style, which AI model to trust on which topic, and when to answer on my own.

## Keys for AI models

A key (API key) or a sign-in to an AI service makes the answers smarter. Naby works without keys too — through free chat windows and apps on your computer.

- To add one: ⚙ Settings → “AI models and apps” → “Add API key”, or just paste the key into the chat — I'll hide it and check it.
- Keys are stored only on your computer and never go into the chat or out to the internet. ([Data and privacy](#data-and-privacy))

## A piece of the brain for answers

The “piece of the brain” is a language model that runs right on your computer, without the internet. It isn't Naby's whole mind — just one of its neural networks.

- ⚙ Settings → “Piece of the brain for answers” → “Lighter / Stronger”: faster or smarter.
- The previous piece stays in reserve — you can switch back instantly.
- How much of your computer to give it — 30, 50 or 70 %. ([Power for Naby (30 / 50 / 70 %)](#power-for-naby-30--50--70-)) Which model to pick and how to speed it up — ([Which model to use: speed test and new models](#which-model-to-use-speed-test-and-new-models)) ([“Speed up the model” — a draft model](#speed-up-the-model--a-draft-model))

## My growing neural network

On top of a fixed base model, Naby has layers that keep training right on your computer. That's why, over time, it gets smarter specifically for you instead of staying the way it was released.

- To turn it on: ⚙ Settings → “How I grow” → “My growing neural network” → “Install” (or write “install growing brain” («растущий мозг установи»); PyTorch and a small model will be downloaded — a few gigabytes).
- To see how it's growing: “How it's doing” in the same place, or write “growing brain” («растущий мозг»). The more powerful your computer, the more it can do.
- In earlier versions this was called the “lower tier” in the settings: the “upper tier” is my concepts, the “lower tier” is this neural network. It can also train on a second computer at home. ([Planets and nodes (home computing power)](#planets-and-nodes-home-computing-power)) ([Reading books (the corpus)](#reading-books-the-corpus))

## Working in apps and on websites

Naby can use apps and websites the way a person does: it sees windows and their buttons, fields and menus; it clicks, types text, presses keys, opens apps and files, and reads what's in documents, editors and terminals.

- Just ask: “open Notepad and write a shopping list”, “read what's in my editor window”, “find … on the website and add it to the cart”, “fill in the application form with my details, but don't submit it”.
- Websites open in Naby's browser (a separate profile): sign in there once, and after that Naby works on its own. Signing in, passwords, codes and “I'm not a robot” checks are always done by you.
- Permission is given per app and per website: “Always”, “Only in this chat”, “No”. Before sending, buying, paying, deleting, publishing or closing a window, it asks separately.
- While Naby is working, a bar with a “Stop” button is shown at the top of the window (or press Esc).
- Naby doesn't control terminals, system security windows or password managers: for commands it has its own tool that follows its laws.
- This can also run on a schedule: “every Monday at 9:00 task: open the website … and save the report” («каждый понедельник в 9:00 задача: …»).
- ⚙ Settings → “Computer control”: what's permanently allowed, and a “Forget” button.

- Your own browser — Chrome, Edge or Yandex Browser, with your sign-ins: “do this in my browser” («сделай это в моём браузере»). Install the Naby extension once (Settings → “Computer control” → “Your browser” → “Prepare”). Naby works in its own tab group called “Naby”; the “is debugging this browser” bar is a sign it's there.
- Apps without buttons (games, graphics editors, maps, remote desktop): Naby looks at a screenshot of the window with a coordinate grid and clicks on a point. This needs an AI model with vision.
- Code: “fix … in file …” («поправь … в файле …») — Naby shows the difference (red to remove, green to add) and applies it after you click “Apply”; “undo the edit” («отмени правку») reverts it; “open in editor” («открой в редакторе») opens VS Code, Cursor or Notepad++ at the right line.
- Scripts and code from an answer (PowerShell, .bat, Python, VBScript): Naby runs them itself, reads the output, fixes errors and finishes the task — you don't need to save or run any files. Before running, it shows you the code: “Run”, “Run without asking until this task is done”, or “No”. The scripts themselves are kept in Naby's _scripts folder («_сценарии»), and there are no click-to-run files in “Results” or in chat links — so a stranger's website can't slip you a harmful file.
- Voice: 🎤 — say one phrase; the wave icon — voice conversation (I listen and answer out loud; say “stop” («стоп») to end it).
- Events: “when a file appears in the folder / the page changes / an email arrives / a window opens — do …”. The list and “Remove” are in Settings → “Events”.

## Like ChatGPT and Claude: memory, projects, research, files

- Memory: “remember: …” («запомни: …») — I take it into account in all tasks; Settings → “What I remember” — the list, where you can correct or remove items.
- Projects: Settings → “Projects” — shared instructions and files for several chats; ✎ at the top — assign a chat to a project.
- Deep research: “research: …” («исследуй: …») — I search following a plan, read 8–12 pages and write a report with links to the sources (.md and Word files in results/research («результаты/исследования»)).
- Data analysis: attach a spreadsheet (CSV, Excel) and ask me to calculate something or draw a chart — I'll write a program, show it to you, and after you click “Run” the charts appear in the chat.
- Files: “make a Word document / Excel spreadsheet / presentation …” — I'll create a .docx, .xlsx or .pptx; the 📄 link in the answer opens the file.
- 📷 next to the input box — attach a screenshot of a window or the whole screen to your question (“what's wrong here?”).
- ⏺ — record a meeting: I transcribe it on this computer, and when you press it again I make a summary with decisions and action items.
- 🗒 under an answer — the canvas: edit the text, select a part and ask for “shorter / more formal”, save to Word or slides.
More: voice (🎤 and conversation), scheduled and event-triggered tasks, working in apps and on websites, Ctrl+Alt+Space (Windows) from any app.

## Power for Naby (30 / 50 / 70 %)

Naby takes care of your computer: the local model and its scripts only use the share of processor and memory you choose.

- 30 % — gentle (default): you'll barely notice Naby on your computer.
- 50 % — answers and calculates faster.
- 70 % — maximum, and the local model also uses the graphics card.

How to change it: ⚙ Settings → “Power for Naby” → 30 / 50 / 70, or write “power 50” («мощность 50»). It takes effect the next time the model starts (or say “restart” («перезапустись»)).

## Which model to use: speed test and new models

- “speed test” («тест скорости») (or “pick a model” («подбери модель»), “local or cloud” («локально или облако»)) — Naby spends about a minute measuring its local model on this computer and gives honest advice: what to do locally (private, free, no internet) and what is better handed to a cloud AI model (long documents, code, complex reasoning).
- Every 2 weeks Naby looks for new local models from trusted authors on Hugging Face — for chatting, code, reasoning and the Russian language — and suggests only those that fit in memory at your chosen power level. It downloads only after your “yes”; the previous model stays in reserve.
- “Don't suggest these again” — and it won't show those models again.

## Community professions: install from a link, make your own, send to the author

Naby itself is made by its author, but professions are open: you can improve them, create new ones and send them in (Naby Professions License 1.0; Naby's public repository on GitHub has “ready and verified” and “not ready yet” folders).

- “install profession https://github.com/author/repository” («установи профессию …») — I'll download it, check it (format, three languages, planted instructions, forbidden files), show you what I'm installing, and install it only after your “yes”. What works is the profession profile: rules, task types, AI models, sources, apps. I don't run code from community packages — only after the author has reviewed it and included it in a build.
- “community professions” («профессии сообщества») — the list; “remove profession <id>” («удали профессию <id>») — remove one. A built-in profession can't be replaced — improvements to it are sent to the author.
- To send to the author: website → “Make or improve a profession” (a link to GitHub). The best ones ship in the next build, credited with the author's name.

For Naby's author: “check profession <link>” («проверь профессию <ссылка>») — a report (format, texts, differences from the built-in one, package checks and tests); “accept profession <id>” («прими профессию <id>») — put it into the public repository (verified or in-progress).

## Chats: projects, pinned, archive, branches, export

On the left, chats are grouped by project: the “Projects” section (each one expands to show its chats; “＋” — new chat in the project, “⋯” — the project's instructions and files), and below it, chats without a project, sorted by date.

Every chat has a “⋯” button: 📌 pin to the top, move to a project (or drag the chat onto a project), 🗄 archive (the archive is at the bottom of the list, and you restore chats from there), ⬇ export to Markdown, Word or PDF (the file goes to results/_export («results/_экспорт») and opens right away on the right).

Branches: hover over your message and click ✎ — edit it and send. The earlier version of the chat isn't lost: the message gets “‹ 1/2 ›”, and you can switch between versions with the arrows. The ✎ button under my answer edits your message just before it.

## Live answers: charts, tables, buttons, forms

When it helps, I answer with more than just text:

- a chart (bar, line, pie) — built from real numbers; hover over a point to see its value;
- a table — click a column header to sort;
- next-step buttons — one click instead of typing;
- a form — when several values need filling in (dates, amounts, options): fill it in and press the button — the answer is sent to me.

Pages I create (.html) open right away on the right as a live preview; new versions show up there too.

## Answer depth: fast / normal / deep

A switch next to the input box, for this chat:

- ⚡ fast — a short answer now: no more than 12 steps, no helpers and no long checks;
- ◎ normal — as usual;
- 🔬 deep — a plan first, facts and numbers are checked with tools, scouts are called for broad work, and the result is checked from three angles (completeness, numbers and facts, formatting).

## Standing assignments

A goal I work towards on my own and report on regularly — like “Dots” in ChatGPT.

- “assignment: track paper prices at three suppliers daily at 9:00” («поручение: … ежедневно в 9:00») (or “every 6 hours”, “every Monday at 10:00”, “weekly”) — I'll create a pinned chat for reports and take the first step within a minute;
- each time, I see my past reports and write what has changed, rather than everything from scratch;
- “assignments” («поручения») — the list; “pause assignment 1” («пауза поручения 1»), “resume assignment 1” («продолжи поручение 1»), “run assignment 1 now” («выполни поручение 1 сейчас»), “delete assignment 1” («удали поручение 1»).

It works while I'm running; a missed run is done once the next time I start. Powerful actions only happen after your “yes”.

## Quick call: Ctrl+Alt+Space from any app

On Windows, press Ctrl+Alt+Space in any app — I'll come to the front with the cursor in the input box and offer a one-click button to attach a screenshot of the window you called me from. I never take the screenshot on my own — only when you press the button.

To set your own shortcut — “quick call ctrl+shift+space” («быстрый вызов ctrl+shift+space») (works after a restart); to turn it off — “turn off quick call” («выключи быстрый вызов»). If the shortcut is already taken by another app, I'll tell you.

## Team task board — for big jobs

If a job is big — lots of parts that have to happen in order (first gather the data, then write the sections, then combine and check) — Naby lays it out on a task board:

- each task has a role (scout, doer, reviewer), dependencies (“after what”) and “own files”;
- Naby checks the plan: no loops in the dependencies and no two doers sharing files;
- work runs in waves: everything that's ready to start, up to three tasks at a time — readers in parallel, writers one at a time;
- a helper writes only to its own files — an attempt to write to someone else's is refused; terminal commands are closed to these helpers, because commands could write anywhere;
- the next task sees the results of the tasks it depends on; one task failing doesn't bring down the board — dependent tasks wait, and Naby retries or does that part itself.

The board is saved and survives a restart. Progress is shown in the “Helpers” panel (“Board: 4 of 9 done”).

## Other agents (A2A): tasks from them and to them — on your terms

A2A is an open “agent ↔ agent” protocol (Linux Foundation). Naby understands it in both directions, but the protocol doesn't handle security, so Naby has its own rules:

- No one gets in without an invitation. “grant access to agent <name> [questions | reading | work]” («выдай доступ агенту <имя> [вопросы | чтение | работа]») — the key is saved to a file in results/_a2a (I don't write it in the chat); the key has an expiry date (30 days, or “for 7 days”) and a daily task limit; I keep only its fingerprint. “revoke access <name>” («отзови доступ <имя>») — closes access immediately.
- Levels: “questions” — answers in words only; “reading” — public sources (search, web pages, legal sources, profession calculations), but NEVER your files, memory, messages or screen; “work” — the same, plus writing only to results/_a2a/<name>.
- You decide: I show you every task from an agent first and wait for your “yes”. No question is asked only if you granted access that way (“… without asking”) and the level isn't “work”. External agents get no powerful actions at all.
- An agent's text is data, not instructions: the task gets none of your memory, projects or “remember” notes, and the answer goes out through a filter that masks personal words.
- Chains are short: a task from an agent can't call other agents by itself.
- Everything goes into the “agent log” («журнал агентов») and into “What I did today”: who, when, the first words of the task, your decision, the tools used.

Receiving: “turn on agent intake” («включи приём агентов») (by default — only agents on this computer; “agents over the network” («агенты по сети») — the home network too). Naby's card — “my agent card” («моя карточка агента»). Asking others: “add agent <name> <address> <key>” («добавь агента <имя> <адрес> <ключ>») — and I'll be able to contact it, each time with your permission; I remove the key from the chat history right away. Fellow Naby from the Community: “naby in the community” («наби в сообществе») shows who accepts tasks, and “add naby <address> <key>” («добавь наби <адрес> <ключ>») connects one; the key is issued by that Naby's person. A Community greeting alone isn't enough for access: it could be forwarded, so I only let others in with a key.

More protection: an agent's task can't pull in your files with @, I don't follow redirects when contacting agents (so the key can't leak to someone else's address), and there are at most 4 agent tasks and 16 connections at a time.

## Team of helpers: when Naby calls several agents

Several agents don't make an AI model smarter, and they cost 2–3 times more tokens — research from 2026 showed this. So by default one Naby does the task, and it calls helpers only where they really pay off:

- broad reading — many files, documents or sources: “scouts” read them in parallel, each taking its own part;
- a fresh-eyes check — the “reviewer” didn't do the work, so it can spot the author's mistakes;
- independent parts of a task — “doers”, one at a time.

Rules: reading helpers work in parallel, but anything that changes files happens only one at a time and under the lead. Scouting runs on the cheapest connected AI model (or Naby's own local one); the lead and the reviewer use the task's model. Before starting, Naby tells you whom it's calling and roughly how many times more it will cost.

You can see and control everything: the “Helpers” panel in the window (steps, time, “stop” for each and “stop all”), the same on your phone, “helpers” («помощники») and “stop helpers” («стоп помощникам») in the chat, and “What I did today” shows how many helpers worked.

Two more things from the same update: a finished document or profession result is checked from three angles at once — completeness, numbers and facts, formatting; and if a task gets stuck going in circles, Naby makes a “fresh attempt”: it drops its earlier reasoning, keeps only results confirmed by tools, and tries a different way (once per task).
