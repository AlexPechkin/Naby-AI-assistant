# License and pricing

## Free for personal use

Naby is **free** for yourself, your family and your studies, with all features included.

## Commercial license

If Naby helps you earn money, you need a one-year commercial license. That covers work as a sole trader or freelancer, use in a company, and any other income.

| License | Price per year |
|---|---|
| Personal commercial: one person, one device | **$10** |
| Company: any size, one device included | **$100** |
| Additional device for a company | **$20** each |

**How to buy (international):**
1. Fill in the short form at [neuralaibaby.tech/commercial.html](https://neuralaibaby.tech/commercial.html) and click **Pay on Boosty**.
2. On Boosty, send your license amount. Boosty shows rubles: $10 = 1,000 ₽, $100 = 10,000 ₽, $20 = 2,000 ₽. Your card is charged in your own currency. Put your e-mail in the donation message.
3. Your license number (NABY-C-XXXX-XXXX) arrives by e-mail within 1–2 business days.
4. Paste the number into the Naby chat. Settings will show “Commercial license activated”.

In Russia, the license is paid by card through Robokassa on the Russian version of the website, and the number arrives automatically.

A license also closes your computer's power to other Nabys in the community.
