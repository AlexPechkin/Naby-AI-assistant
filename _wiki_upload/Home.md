# Naby Wiki

**Naby is an AI assistant that lives on your computer.** You don't need to know anything about neural networks. Say what you need, and Naby picks the models, tools and programs for the job: a strong cloud model where it pays off, a local model where privacy and cost matter more.

## Why Naby

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔒 **Your data stays with you** | Memory, chats and documents are stored on your PC and encrypted. Private tasks run on the local model. Before anything goes to a cloud model, names and personal details are replaced with placeholders. |
| 💸 **Lower cost** | Each task goes to the cheapest model that can do it well. Before a big job, Naby tells you what it will cost. Simple tasks run free on your own computer. |
| 🧩 **140 professions** | Lawyer, accountant, engineer, doctor, teacher, designer, programmer, scientist and many more. Naby adjusts its rules, task types, models, sources and programs to your work. |
| 🛠 **Improves itself** | Ask for what's missing and Naby adds it. Once a week it reads GitHub, forums and news from your field. It updates itself, and every update is signed. |
| 🤝 **A team when it pays off** | Read-only scouts work in parallel, a reviewer checks with fresh eyes, and big jobs go on a task board. Other agents can connect over A2A, only on your terms. |
