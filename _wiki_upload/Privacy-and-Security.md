# Privacy and security

## Your data

- **Everything lives on your computer.** Memory, conversations, documents and settings stay on your PC, and Naby's memory is encrypted.
- **Masking before the cloud.** Before anything is sent to an external neural network, names and personal details are replaced with placeholders.
- **A log of everything sent out.** “What I did today” shows where data went and how many characters, without the text itself.
- **Keys and passwords stay with you.** API keys never appear in the chat or leave your PC. Naby never types or sees passwords: on a login form it calls your password manager instead.
- **Private tasks stay local.** In a profession's private mode 🔒, and whenever someone else's personal data appears, Naby works only with the local model.

## Protection

| Threat | What Naby does |
|---|---|
| Hidden instructions in emails, pages or files | Detects and ignores them. For the rest of the task, every important step needs your yes. |
| A third-party skill or plugin | Scans it before installing (“check skill …”) and installs it only as a draft that you approve. |
| A program disguised as a document | Never opens it and moves it to a quarantine folder. |
| Accidental deletion | Deleted items go to Naby's trash for 30 days, and terminal commands cannot delete. |
| Memory poisoning | Only what you said or confirmed can enter memory. Suspicious entries go to quarantine. |
| Other agents (A2A) | Only by invitation key, with a level and an expiry date. Every task needs your yes, and your files and memory are always closed to agents. |
| Community professions | Only the profile is installed. Package code from the community is never run. |
| Fake updates | Every update is signed by the author and verified before it is installed. |

## Permissions

- For every program and website: **Always / This conversation only / No**.
- Sending, buying, paying, deleting, publishing and closing windows are confirmed separately every time.
- While Naby works, a bar with a **Stop** button is visible. Esc stops it too.

## Naby's laws

These are sealed in the core and cannot be changed:

0. Do not harm humanity.
1. Do not harm your person and their family: their health, data, money or privacy.
2. Obey your person, but say honestly when it disagrees.
3. Protect itself.
4. Always say honestly what it is, and never pretend to be human.
